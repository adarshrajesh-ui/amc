       Quant Trader Interview - Quantitative Research 
    

  


   
     
                  
       
        Anonymous interview candidate in Bala Cynwyd 
    


     
      Interviewed:
      December
      2023
    


     
      Date Submitted:
      Mar 14, 2025
    

  


   
   
     
       
         Outcome

         
             No Offer

      

      

       
         Interview Source

         
             Applied Online

      

      

       
         Length of Process

         
             1-2 months

      

      

    

  


   
   
     
       
         Application

         
       
               Phone Interview

               1 on 1 Interview

               Presentation

               Skills Test

          

  

      


       
         Interview

         
             First a quantitative test, mostly probability / logic / calculus questions. Then a couple of technical interviews, where they ask tricky probability/stats/puzzle questions, and a coding interview that was relatively easy compared to others in the industry. Then a take home data science project where you are asked to predict volatilty for the next month using historical data. Finally virtual onsite - 5 interviews with various members of the team to assess your fit. The final interview questions are not too hard, but you need to be very sharp and provide clear solutions quickly so you have time to discuss them afterwards. Some market making (minimize bid-ask spread given constraints), betting (e.g. Kelly fraction), and quick probability estimation questions in final interview - make sure to study these types of questions.

      

      


       
         Interview Questions

         
       
               The market making question - minimize a bid ask spread. I didn't know what a bid-ask spread was before that question.
