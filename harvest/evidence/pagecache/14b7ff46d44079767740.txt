     Dear LinkedIn connections and fellow techies,

I recently had the chance to attempt an Online Coding Assessment for the Graduate Software Development role at Susquehanna, and I’d love to share some insights from the experience.
While the outcome didn’t go my way this time, the process itself was a valuable learning curve that helped me sharpen my problem-solving approach.

💻 Coding Assessment (70 minutes)
There were a total of 4 algorithmic questions in the test, covering different problem-solving areas such as:
🔹 Identifying zigzag patterns in consecutive triples of an array
🔹 Handling update and query operations across two arrays
🔹 Array-based logical reasoning
🔹 Optimisation under constraints

At first glance, the problems felt challenging. But breaking them down step by step reminded me how important it is to stay calm and think systematically under timed pressure.

📩 Final Outcome
Even though I didn’t progress further this time, I’m grateful for the experience. It reinforced my coding fundamentals, improved my ability to write efficient solutions under time constraints, and gave me a benchmark for future assessments.

💡 Advice for anyone preparing:
✅ Practice solving problems with both logic and efficiency in mind
✅ Use mock timed sessions to build confidence
✅ Treat every assessment as a growth opportunity, not just a pass/fail outcome

Every attempt adds to your toolkit. Onwards and upwards 🚀
