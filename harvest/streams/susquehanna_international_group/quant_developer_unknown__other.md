*EN:* Question 2 is just computing the minimum vertical distance between a "#" (or a "-" at the bottom) and the nearest "F". Take the minimum of all those minima to get a distance d. Then extract all the "F" positions and rewrite them as "-". Finally rewrite the cell at the new coordinate (x, y+d) corresponding to each old coordinate (x, y) as "F".
