*English:* A frog wants to jump from (0,0) to (7,4); each jump can only be one square to the right or one square up. The frog is unwilling to jump three times in a row in the same direction. How many different jump sequences are there?

*Reported answer:* 30. Poster gives two derivations: a case count ('最终答案合计情况1的(1+6+9+2)和情况2的3*(2+2) 共30种'), and a composition count ('分拆4个R字符拆成2段有1种,拆成3段有3种,拆成4段有1种;7个U字符拆成4段有4种,拆成5段有10种;所以答案是3 * 4 + 2 * 1 * 4 + 1 * 10 = 30'). A commenter adds a DP: dp[i][j] = dp[i-1][j-1] + dp[i-1][j-2] + dp[i-2][j-1] + dp[i-2][j-2], 边界条件是 dp[0][0] = 2, dp[1][0] = dp[2][0] = dp[0][1] = dp[0][2] = 1
