### What candidates say about interviewing at Hudson River Trading

**Interview process & format** – The interview process is generally lengthy and may involve multiple stages, including online assessments, phone screenings, and panel interviews. Candidates have noted that interviews can range from technical coding assessments to discussions about market knowledge and behavioral questions.
