### **What candidates say about interviewing at Five Rings**

**Interview process & format** — Candidates report a rigorous interview structure featuring an online assessment followed by multiple technical interviews. Most assessments focus heavily on math, probability, and quick estimations.

**Interview timelines** — Several candidates experienced prompt feedback after the online assessment, with some receiving rejections within minutes of completion, indicating a fast-paced hiring process.

**Candidate experience** — Many found the interviewers to be professional and friendly, though the rapid-fire questioning style and the high difficulty of the assessments left some feeling stressed and unprepared. Overall, while the atmosphere was supportive, the interview challenges were notably tough.

I applied online.The process took 2 months.I interviewed at Five Rings

Interview

1st round HR zoom call, short behavioral followed by~10 questions, 30 seconds each, fast-paced. 2nd, 3rd, 4th rounds consist of a question with 3-4 follow-ups. You get a good amount of time (like 50 mins total), and you can ask your interviewer questions.

*   Is it hard to get hired as a Trading Intern at Five Rings?Trading Intern applicants have rated the interview process at Five Rings with 3.7 out of 5 (where 5 is the highest level of difficulty) and assessed their interview experience as 40% positive. To compare, the company-average is 35.2% positive. This is according to Glassdoor user ratings. 
*   How long does it take to get hired as a Trading Intern at Five Rings?Candidates applying for Trading Intern roles take an average of 33 days to get hired, when considering 7 user submitted interviews for this role. To compare, the hiring process at Five Rings overall takes an average of 19 days.
