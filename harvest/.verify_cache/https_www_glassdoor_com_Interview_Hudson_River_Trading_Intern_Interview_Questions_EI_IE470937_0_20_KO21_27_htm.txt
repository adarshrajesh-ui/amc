I applied online.The process took 2 weeks.I interviewed at Hudson River Trading (New York, NY) in Oct 2022
Interview
Few rounds of interview after a coding assessment. Then on-site. The coding assessment is difficult (4 questions in 90 minutes) and the rounds of interview are difficult as well (one brain-teaser like interview and one programming one). I didn't make it to the on-site because of the programming interview.
Interview questions [1]

Non disclosure agreement, but the brainteasers are probability based (with kind of intuition to bring). LC hard for the programming !

2w
Someone told me they had applied for over 100 jobs with no interviews. They blamed the system and the companies. I asked, Have you looked at your resume from a recruiter's point of view?Often, the issue is not the market but how you present yourself. A resume that looks fine to you might not speak to hiring teams.
Before assuming the system is broken, take a second look. Is your resume doing its job? Sometimes a simple shift in strategy can make all the difference.
Are you evamping that resume?
