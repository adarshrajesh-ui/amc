Application
I applied online.The process took 2 weeks.I interviewed at Five Rings in 3/1/2024
Interview
Initial call with recruiter involved a few behavioral questions and then \~10 rapid fire stats and probability questions. The time allotted for each question varied from 20 seconds to 90 seconds, so it required very quick thinking. I think most of the questions are solvable in the time given (if you've seen the problem before or immediately see the strategy), but some of them seem to require some amount of estimation.
The recruiter was very nice and made it a nice conversation. Didn't make it through to next round unfortunately.

Question 1
