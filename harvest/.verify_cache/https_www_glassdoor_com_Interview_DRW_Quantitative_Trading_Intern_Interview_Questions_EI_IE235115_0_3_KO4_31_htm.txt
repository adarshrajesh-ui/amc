### What candidates say about interviewing at DRW

**Interview process & format** – Candidates experience a multi-stage process that often includes technical assessments, behavioral interviews, and coding challenges. The process typically consists of an online assessment followed by interviews that delve into technical skills, problem-solving abilities, and cultural fit.

**Candidate experience** – The general atmosphere is described as friendly and collaborative, although some candidates report poor communication and follow-up. Concerns about being "ghosted" after promising interviews are common, leading to frustrations about the lack of transparency.

**Interview timelines** – While some candidates appreciate the structured approach and clarity on expectations, others note delays in the hiring process and a lack of updates, which can create uncertainties about their application status.
