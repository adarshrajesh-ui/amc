## DRW interviews FAQs
* Is it hard to get hired at DRW?Glassdoor users rated their interview experience at[DRW](https://www.glassdoor.com/Overview/Working-at-DRW-EI_IE235115.11,14.htm)as 44.3% positive with a difficulty rating score of 3.32 out of 5 (where 5 is the highest level of difficulty). Candidates interviewing for[Junior Trading Analyst](https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/DRW-Junior-Trading-Analyst-Interview-Questions-EI_IE235115.0,3_KO4,26.htm)and[Desktop Systems Engineer](https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/DRW-Desktop-Systems-Engineer-Interview-Questions-EI_IE235115.0,3_KO4,28.htm)rated their interviews as the hardest, whereas interviews for[Quantitative Analyst Intern](https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/DRW-Quantitative-Analyst-Intern-Interview-Questions-EI_IE235115.0,3_KO4,31.htm)and[Quant Analyst Intern](https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/DRW-Quant-Analyst-Intern-Interview-Questions-EI_IE235115.0,3_KO4,24.htm)roles were rated as the easiest.
* How long does it take to get hired at DRW?The hiring process at[DRW](https://www.glassdoor.com/Overview/Working-at-DRW-EI_IE235115.11,14.htm)takes an average of 20.04 days when considering 300 user submitted interviews across all job titles. Candidates applying for[Quant](https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/DRW-Quant-Interview-Questions-EI_IE235115.0,3_KO4,9.htm)had the quickest hiring process (on average 1 day), whereas[Commodities Trader](https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/DRW-Commodities-Trader-Interview-Questions-EI_IE235115.0,3_KO4,22.htm)roles had the slowest hiring process (on average 90 days).
* What is the interview process like at DRW?Common stages of the[interview process at DRW](https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/DRW-Interview-Questions-E235115.htm)according to 300 Glassdoor interviews include:
Phone interview: 26%
One on one interview: 21.78%
Skills test: 15.46%
Presentation: 11.48%
Group panel interview: 7.49%
IQ intelligence test: 5.85%
Personality test: 4.92%
Background check: 3.04%
Other: 2.11%
Drug test: 1.87%
