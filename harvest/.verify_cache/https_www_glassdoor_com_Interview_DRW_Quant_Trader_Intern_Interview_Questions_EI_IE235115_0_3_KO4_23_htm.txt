### **What candidates say about interviewing at DRW**

**Interview process & format** – Candidates experienced a structured process with multiple stages, including take-home tasks, technical interviews, and behavioral assessments. Roles like Software Engineer and Quant Trader had a mix of coding challenges and market-related questions.

**Candidate experience** – Most candidates described the interviewers as friendly and professional. However, some reported poor communication, with instances of being ghosted after interviews, leading to frustration.

**Interview difficulty** – The difficulty level varies by role, with many candidates finding assessments challenging, especially for quantitative positions. Several mentioned needing solid preparation to succeed, particularly in probability and statistics.

**Timelines** – Some candidates noted delays in communication regarding interview outcomes, which contributed to a sense of uncertainty during the hiring process.

## DRW interviews FAQs

*   Is it hard to get hired as a Quant Trader Intern at DRW?Quant Trader Intern applicants have rated the interview process at DRW with 3.5 out of 5 (where 5 is the highest level of difficulty) and assessed their interview experience as 91% positive. To compare, the company-average is 45.6% positive. This is according to Glassdoor user ratings. 
*   How long does it take to get hired as a Quant Trader Intern at DRW?Candidates applying for Quant Trader Intern roles take an average of 10 days to get hired, when considering 11 user submitted interviews for this role. To compare, the hiring process at DRW overall takes an average of 19 days. 
*   What is the interview process like for a Quant Trader Intern at DRW?Common stages of the interview process at DRW as a Quant Trader Intern according to 11 Glassdoor interviews include:Phone interview: 40%

Skills test: 20%

Drug test: 10%

Presentation: 10%

Background check: 10%

IQ intelligence test: 10%
