<p class="wp-block-paragraph"> <strong>DE Shaw </strong>is a multinational investment management firm, the company is known for developing complicated mathematical models and sophisticated computer programs to exploit anomalies in the market. DE Shaw visited our campus in the month of July for the academic year 2020-21 for intern hires with the profile of Software Development Engineer.  </p>



<ul class="wp-block-list"><li><strong>Job Profile:</strong> Software Development Engineer Intern</li><li><strong>Eligibility:</strong> Open for CSE, ECE and EE of B Tech with no elimination based on CGPA.</li><li><strong>Location:</strong> Mumbai</li></ul>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Selection Procedure:&nbsp;</strong></h4>



<ul class="wp-block-list"><li>Online Coding Test</li><li>Technical/ Domain based Interview</li></ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A special thanks to <strong>Shivangi Dubey</strong> for sharing her experience.</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Online test:</strong></h4>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The test consist of 26 MCQ and 2 coding questions with a time duration of 95 minutes. MCQ PART:</p>



<ol class="wp-block-list"><li><em>Aptitude</em> <em>Section</em> (14 MCQs, 28 minutes):&nbsp;</li></ol>



<ul class="wp-block-list"><li> Quantitative. Examples: Probability, Permutation &amp; Combination, Profit &amp; Loss, speed-distance-time, etc.</li><li>Logical reasoning and data interpretation. There were 2 passages with a couple questions based on each.</li></ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>&nbsp;</em>2. <em>Technical Section</em> (12 MCQs, 17 minutes):</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list"><li> Academic subjects based. Examples: Data structure and algorithms, SQL, etc.&nbsp;</li><li> Guess the output of given pseudo code/algorithms.</li><li>Debugging. A pseudo code and expected output would be given. Candidates had to find and fix the bugs to achieve the given output.</li></ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">CODING PART (2 questions, 50 minutes):&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"> First question was given with time limit of 20 minutes latter one with 30 minutes.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">NOTES:&nbsp;</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list"><li>The marking scheme for MCQs was +3 for the correct answer and -1 for incorrect.&nbsp;</li><li> Left over time (if any) from one section was not carried over to the next.</li></ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The MCQs of aptitude and coding were tricky and of decent difficulty. The two coding questions of the coding round were from dynamic programming of competitive programming.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">14 out of 100+ students who appeared for the test were shortlisted for further technical interviews.</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong><strong>Technical/ Domain based interview</strong></strong>:</h4>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There were two face-to-face technical interviews, the first one was of about 1 hr and I was asked to solve 3 coding questions on the HackerRank platform.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">6 out of 14 students moved to the second technical round.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The second round lasted for 1.5 hrs and similarly had 2 questions like first-round except for the fact that the questions in this round were a bit more complex and more modifications were asked to perform to the codes.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">2 out of 6 students were finally offered the internship.</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Tips:</strong> </h4>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The key factor for my selection would be practice. I have been practicing a lot on Leetcode and Codeforces. It helps you sharpen your instincts and once you practice enough you would know what approach might fail after actually writing its code.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The only thing you need to maintain is consistency, attempt as many contests you can as it will improve your speed and accuracy to write code that can pass in single attempt. And once you have a good grip on contests, follow Leetcode rigorously.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Good luck for your interviews!</p>
