Process

I had an OA (2 questions), then a Final Round. The Final Round consisted of three 60-minute technical interviews and three rounds of behavioral interviews, only if you passed the technical round. The technical final involved 2 LeetCode-style questions and 1 debugging question.

Great work culture, transparent, and super talented

Software Engineer Intern • Former Employee
New York, New York, United States
October 9, 2023
Pros: Almost everyone on my team had a PhD, and everyone was really smart. Two Sigma does a good job letting you explore around the company and learn. I really appreciate the culture of learning and teamwork at Two Sigma. Two Sigma focuses a lot on diversity as well. I didn't notice any direct impact on work/experience from the CEO's feuding. Also, the work-life balance was really good (9-5).
Cons: Two Sigma has been having headcount issues recently. Also, I wish the pay progression was comparable to some of the firms in the industry.
