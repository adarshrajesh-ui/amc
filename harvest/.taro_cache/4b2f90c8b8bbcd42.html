    DRW Interview Questions

    Counting Elements

    Easy

    Given an array of integers, count how many elements have a corresponding element that is exactly one greater. For example, if the array contains 1, 2, and 3, both 1 (because 2 is present) and 2 (because 3 is present) would be counted.

    ArraysHash Table

    Subsequence of Size K With the Largest Even Sum

    Medium

    Find a subsequence of size k from a given array such that the sum of the subsequence's elements is even and maximized. Return the largest even sum achievable.

    ArraysGreedy Algorithms

    Maximize Score After Pair Deletions

    Medium

    You are given a list of numbers, and you can delete pairs of numbers that have a difference of one. Your goal is to maximize the total sum of the numbers you keep after performing these deletions.

    ArraysGreedy Algorithms
