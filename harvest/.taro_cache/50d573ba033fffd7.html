Intern Software Developer Interview Experience - Sydney, Australia

May 1, 2021
Positive ExperienceGot Offer

Process

The first round was a HackerRank test, which was fairly straightforward.

The second round was a one-on-one HackerRank-style question over the phone. I can't recall the specifics, but it involved a grid and likely a Breadth-First Search (BFS).

The third round was a Zoom interview. Part one focused on a systems design question about retrieving product data from a server and implementing a low-latency system for "buy low, sell high." Part two was another HackerRank-style question, this time about Battleships.

Overall, the interviewers were very nice and accommodating with small mistakes.

Questions

Possible strategies for a battleship player.
