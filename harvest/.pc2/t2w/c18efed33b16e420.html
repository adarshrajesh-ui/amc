At this point TS2000i has nothing to do with Tradestation. (Well, vice versa, actually.) They only support TS8.1 which is only sold by subscription. So what you enter will depend on your datafeed and middleware. What are you using?

Most vendors I've seen use ER2 for the mini russel 2000, although I think esignal uses AB for some reason, and Qcharts used to use AQ. The major difference is in the month coding: Esignal uses " #F" for the continuous contract, some have a space before the "M6", some don't. Metaserver RT for IB has a special tool to build the symbol from IB which seemed to work OK.
