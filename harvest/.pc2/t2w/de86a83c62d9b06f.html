Following the move to the new site, I'd like to take this opportunity to propose some changes to the forums, the idea of this thread is to get everyone's feedback on those changes and to let you all make your own suggestions on how we can improve the categorisation of the forums.

So to start the conversation going, here are my own thoughts on changes we could make. Mostly it's renaming, but there's a bit of splitting and merging as well..
  • Split out "Algorithmic Trading" from "Trading Systems" - the former being discussion on programming methods, the latter being the systems themselves .
  • Rename "Data Feeds" to "Data Sets & Feeds" to reflect the importance of good quality data sets for algorithmic traders.
  • Merge "Forex Brokers" into "Brokerages" and rename to simply "Brokers" - as it's more commonly referred to.
  • Rename Stocks to Stocks & ETFs - and move any ETF threads there.
  • Rename the "Futures & Options" forum to just "Options" and move futures threads into the appropriate market related forum, eg. Indices, Commodities etc.
  • Rename "Indices" to "Index Futures" to emphasise that Futures is now merged into these other forums.
  • Rename "Commodities & Money Markets" to ‘Commodity Futures & Money Markets" - for similar reasons.
  • Close the "Fixed Odds" forum - association with Binary Betting is toxic and these could be moved into General Trading Chat
  • Rename "Economic & Fundamental Analysis" to "News (or Political), Economic & Fundamental Analysis"
  • Rename "Daily Analysis" to "Daily Market Analysis"
  • Rename "Trading Systems" to "Trading Systems & Indicators"
If you agree/disagree or have your own suggestions, please spend a couple of minutes reviews the list of forums; and please do share your own thoughts.
