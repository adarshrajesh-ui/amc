In Venn diagram speak, the items in the intersection of the sets defined by these two statements may not be that big so chances of success are slim. You could be shown the best strategy in the world and it may not work for you.

As you can spell "I'm" (I can't STAND it when people use "im"), I'll continue. Why not take the methods you already use and work out why they are failing? Work out when they work and when you're best off staying out. For instance, a trend following system will be blown out of the water when the market is ranging so you'd need to figure out how to tell when it's ranging... good luck. 🙂
