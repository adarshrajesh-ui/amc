bigears et al -

Here is my opinion on this product (which I first posted under 'Trading Resources' thread before I saw this one):

Hi Bigears -

I have actually just receieved my copy this week from T2W store - and I'm not selling!

18 traders talk openly , thoroughly and candidly about how they started, what they trade and what methodologies/strategies they use.

I have only viewed the first few interviews (on the first CD) so far. These are very interesting, 20-25 min 'chats' with traders from all walks of life. Most are sat at their desks/trading stations during the interview.

The first CD includes a group of traders who use (pay for) the facilities of a trading room set up in a Surrey Country Club. Many others are traders sat at home using their own equipment.

I think the whole set (3 CDs) will prove to be very inspiring and educational material. Personally, I can easliy relate to the points made and issues raised - the learning curve, (including the inevitable big hit early on), the development of trading and business plans, and the importance of risk/money management to ultimate success.

There is over 7 hours of material here - I plan to watch a couple of interviews each day during my lunch breaks, over the next 2 weeks or so.

If you want to see real self-employed/self-funded traders talking about this business in a real-time trading environment, this product is well worth £37.

IMO.
