thanks for your reply GammaJammer.

I guess since I still find trading jobs interest me more than actuarial job (i just finished my actuarial science degree), I'll keep following the markets. so if i get another interview i can be better prepared and i may sound wittier.

🙂 You are so right that interview can be very random... and would you give me some examples of the sort of things i should say if they ask me what i can add to the firm?

GammaJammer said:
Have an idea before you go into the interview of a few key things;

1) who the company are - what their priorities are;
2) What the industry is about - future trends etc
3) What YOU can add to this

This stuff really isn't rocket science. And first impressions are hard to get past - interviews are more random than you think.

As for whether it's harder being a girl, I'm not one, so I can't really speak with authority but statistics obviously bear this out so I can't really argue with that.

At the end of the day, interviews are usually about personalities. And the smaller the team, the more important it is that a new hire is a good personal fit for a firm.

Hope that helps.

GJ
