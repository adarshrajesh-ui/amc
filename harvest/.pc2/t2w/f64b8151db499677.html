I'll be trading a funded prop firm account with risk capital of $2,200.00. My forward testing of everything I put together is going very close to the back testing I did. Which in my experience and also viewing others experiences is a pretty rare, yet promising thing. Extremely aware of market context and bias direction, neither of which I am relying on as an edge or advantage to make what I am doing work. In other words what I am doing does not solely rely on any of those things to work, but I still can use that information to take higher quality trades and add more probability to my trades, of course. Now in extreme circumstances like the market is limiting down, that of course that could be an exception, but than I would just not place trades if there wasn't a clear edge. The point is my biggest strength is understanding context, overall directional probability and stability of the given market.

I've already received pay outs from prop firms , have one currently pending and another lined up for next month. Would like assistance, help or some type of partnership though to further take advantage of the information, concepts and tools I am using, which are quite extensive and far from one dimensional.

More than willing to trade real time with someone as well though voice, screen share and etc if they are seriously interested in working with me. In lieu of that though or in the mean time wanted to pass another evaluation(which is done) and pick one funded account specifically to track on here, so people can come to the understanding that there's actually something tangible here with a lot of potential, as oppose to just another person talking big and chasing a dream.

Will start posting results at the minimum soon, if not also individual trades when time allows. Please understand though I'll be trading other accounts while also trading this one, which I am relying on as large part of my income currently. So, those will take precedence and may only allow me to post the results here and not always real time trades.
