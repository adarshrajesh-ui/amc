Update for London & Euro Traders as of Dec 18

I contacted FTMO and to their credit, they responded not once
but twice, to my inquiries, asking if they supported trading the
S&P 500 Futures. Both times, it was human beings who responded
and they spoke English and were responsive, however the bottom line
seems to be that they do NOT provide have access to CME data for
the front month of the S&P 500 Futures. What seems to be the case
(in my opinion) is that they provide a CFD (Contract for Difference)
instead, which I do not want. So I thanked them and will move on
to another vendor

To remind readers, I am interested in trading only the S&P 500 Futures
AND I am hoping to find a vendor who will provide "live" customer support
(NOT an AI Bot, but a real living breathing human being). Why? well
frankly I am not a fan of AI. In my opinion trying to obtain a solution to a
problem by talking to an AI agent is like talking to a small child. It is
frustrating.

Ultimately I intend to document my journey in a series of articles and
(perhaps) include my experiences along the way in a book I am working
on that will be published by Wiley.
