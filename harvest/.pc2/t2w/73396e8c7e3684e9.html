Let me expand.

You’ll find although the rights rest with the author, actually translating that into any meaningful leverage is largely impossible.

While quoting of a source for anything reproduced with permission is required de facto, this is rarely enforced or enforceable except in extreme conditions where there is clear evidence of abuse or deceit. There is simply too much to police, so it doesn’t get policed - unless screaming at you it is. 😆

Quite apart from difficulty in establishing ‘ownership’ of a name (which is also a ‘nick’ for instance) even if it were copyrighted or trademarked and had been clearly denoted as such during its usage, and the content associated with it, to the degree required in law, there is also the issue of subsequent requests for changes, modifications or removals being considered ‘vexatious’ and therefore contrary to the rights of the publication/site/business.

Equally, not having trademark or copyright doesn’t mean you don’t own the intellectual rights.
