Affirmer,
I am a 'Gold' member and agree with driller killer and timaru69, with the following comments:
1. They don't do P&F charts - not an issue for me but it might be for some.
2. Beware of inconsistencies in data over different time periods. I've never understood this. (E.g. the open on xyz stock might vary from 5 min to hourly charts).
3. Beware of opening too many different applet windows at once. There's a tendency for them to freeze. However, this may well say more about my computer than it does about prophet.net!
4. Time & Sales is an 'Add On' extra at $7.95 p/m and is not included as standard in any of the memberships. Ditto for Naz Level II data.
HTH,
Tim.
