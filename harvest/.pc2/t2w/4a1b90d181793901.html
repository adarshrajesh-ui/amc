CC;
Futex just do not work on the basis on the basis of telling trainees and traders what they are allowed to trade. We work with the individual to find the markets that suit him best. We trade Bunds, Bobl, Shatz, FTSE, Dax, Cac, Gold, Oil, FX futures, FX cash, T-Notes, T-Bonds, etc etc.
Futex is a Prop Trading House - it is not an arcade business model, based on round trips and bums on seats. We are highly successful because our prime objective is Trader Profitability.
As for connectivity: the profits we realise on news related (e.g. fed discount rate cut) are often substantial. Thats a combination of getting filled and being Wired-In to the news wires in a top quality Professional way.
regards
Futex
