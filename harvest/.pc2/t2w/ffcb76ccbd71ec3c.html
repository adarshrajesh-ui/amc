You should be able to easily do it through a good broker. Ask them to change up sterling into cash euros and then wire the money out to your account in Spain. But the account has to be in your name otherwise they won't do it, money laundering etc.

Whatever you do you want to see the costs involved with all possible routes (broker, the place in Holborn, other companies etc) because doing business with one over another company could save you a lot of money.
