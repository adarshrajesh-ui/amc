Hi all,

I'm new to this site and would like to pick the brains of some of you wonderful people.

I have had an interview for a junior stockbroker role recently, at the interview i was told to do a fasttrack CISI certificate 1 and 2 course. On completing this i was told i would be guaranteed a role. The person who told me this was the interviewer at the recruitment consultancy. He mentioned he knew a chap who could teach me at a reduced price of around £670. Does this sound too good to be true? The PROMISE of a job is very luring. Has anyone heard of instances such as this in the past?

Also with some digging around i found out i could actually buy these course booklets, mock exams etc at a very much reduced rate. Is it possible to actually learn and successfully pass these courses when studying on your own? Or is it a must to attend these week courses? The thing is, i would struggle to afford the £700, and if it is possible to study on your own via distance learning i would much rather take this route.

I would very much appreciate any feedback. Thanks for reading!

There are a lot of companies offering the promise of a job after you pay for training. Some have turned out to be very dodgy. For example GTEX Global and City Financial Traders who were offering something like this seem to have disappeared leaving some unhappy customers.

Maybe that's not always the case but you should do as much due diligence as possible eg how long have they been incorporated, do they have a proper address (not just a virtual office), who is behind the company etc etc.
