 Quantam Invention Interview Experience for Internship

 Last Updated : 26 Aug, 2022 

 I've applied through LinkedIn for an internship in C/C++ in Quantum Invention Kolkata.
 After your resume will get shortlist, You'll get a call from HR of QI for the 1st round which is the MCQ round,
 You'll get a Gmeet link and then hr will send a GForm link of Test.
 You have to keep on your video during the test.
 Once you qualify you'll get a mail for the 2nd round which is the technical round.
 Questions will be asked from your resume, what you've mentioned in resume like Project, Interests, Skills etc.
 You've to write some code in notepad of C++, OOPs.
 Once you qualify this round you'll get mail for HR round and get selected.
