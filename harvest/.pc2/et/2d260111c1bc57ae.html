Try to look at a previous job you had and something that may have been a weakness in it, but would be considered a strength to the new career you are looking for. Maybe the new job is one in which you have to be very thorough with your work. Say something like well one weakness I had at my last job was that I was very thorough with my work, and the job demanded less time on a wide variety of tasks. You want to make it sound a little better than that. Every interview I ever went into, I have been asked this question. So expect it to come up every time. If you have no previous work experience, then apply it to your schooling.
