ROUTE 7: CRITERION CONTAMINATION -- a third inflation mechanism, not reverse causation
==============================================================================
  sadikova2024 decomposed the 6-item Kandel-Davies scale under a causal sleep gain.
  Two items are fatigue/sleep ('too tired to do things', 'trouble going to sleep or
  staying asleep'); four are mood (unhappy/sad/depressed, hopeless, nervous, worrying).
    total score ITT 2 y : -0.26 (-0.50, -0.02)  SIGNIFICANT
    fatigue cluster     : -0.91 (-1.45, -0.38)  SIGNIFICANT, 3.5x the total
    mood cluster        : -0.03 (-0.09,  0.02)  NULL
  If the total is the 6-item mean, the 2 fatigue items contribute (2/6)*fatigue:
    fatigue contribution to the total = (2/6) * -0.91 = -0.3033
    that is 117% of the observed total effect of -0.26
    ASSUMPTION: 'Mood' in Table 2 is the remaining 4 items. If it is item c alone the
    exact share shifts, but it cannot fall much below 100% while mood is null.
  -> essentially the ENTIRE causal effect on the depression SCALE is the scale
     re-measuring the exposure. Independently replicated by berger2026_start in the
     same cohort by difference-in-differences, and predicted by talbot2010's authors
     ('The POMS may indicate greater mood disturbance in part because some of its
     scales overlap with sleepiness, such as fatigue and vigor').
