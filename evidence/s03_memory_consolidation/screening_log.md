# Screening log — shard `s03_memory_consolidation`

**Domain:** sleep-dependent memory consolidation and encoding; the "learning loss" channel (c) —
knowledge and skill never encoded because consolidation/encoding was impaired during three years of
schooling.

**Records screened: 58. Included: 24. Excluded: 34.**

Every identifier below was obtained from a PubMed E-utilities search or Crossref response during this
session — none was recalled from memory. Identifiers on *included* records were additionally verified
against both Crossref and PubMed with the title checked for a match (see each record's `verification`
block). Identifiers on *excluded* records are as returned by the PubMed search that surfaced them.

Search strategy: iterative PubMed E-utilities queries across the six required extraction areas, plus
Crossref title search for the Psychological Bulletin meta-analysis that is not PubMed-indexed under the
terms given in the task, plus citation-chasing from the full texts of `campbell2021`, `james2023`,
`lo2016` and `kumral2023`. Full text was pursued via Europe PMC `fullTextXML`, NCBI PMC, publisher
pages and bioRxiv/PsyArXiv preprints in that order.

## Identifier verification failures

**None.** All 24 included records resolved on both Crossref and PubMed with matching titles. No
identifier had to be set to `null`, and no record required `access_tier: secondhand`.

Two ancillary identifiers were also verified during the work and are recorded where used:

- `10.1101/2022.07.18.500433` — bioRxiv preprint of `kumral2023`, verified via Crossref
  (type `posted-content`, posted 2022-07-19). Used to obtain the pooled estimates, heterogeneity and
  publication-bias statistics that the paywalled journal version's abstract omits.
- `10.31234/osf.io/r2an6` — accepted preprint of `berres2021`, used to obtain confidence intervals,
  heterogeneity and the moderator analyses.

One reference could **not** be verified and is therefore quoted but not extracted:

- "Ujma (2022)", cited inside `kumral2023` as a PET-PEESE reanalysis that reduces the spindle–memory
  correlation to insignificance. Returned no PubMed hits on author or topic search. Reported in
  `kumral2023`'s `notes` strictly as quoted by Kumral et al., with no effect record created and no
  number attributed to it.
