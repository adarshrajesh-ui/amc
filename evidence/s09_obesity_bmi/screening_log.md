## Notes on retrieval difficulties

- **Chen 2008** carries no abstract in PubMed or Europe PMC ("No abstract provided"). Wiley's
  `pdfdirect` endpoint returned an HTML interstitial rather than the PDF. The numbers were obtained
  from the retrieved publisher full-text HTML, from which the verbatim sentences in the YAML
  `quote` fields are taken. `access_tier: full_text`.
- **Markwald 2013** (PMC3619301), **Depner 2019** (PMC12798825), **Widome 2023** (PMC10319406) and
  **Sokol 2020** (PMC7103530) returned zero-byte responses from the Europe PMC `fullTextXML`
  endpoint and a reCAPTCHA wall from `pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov`. For Markwald, Depner and Widome the
  full text (including the Depner body-weight paragraph and the Widome Table 2 difference-in-
  differences row) was retrieved from the publisher, and those verbatim strings are what appear in
  the `quote` fields. For Sokol only the abstract-level text was obtainable, so its forward-path
  null is recorded with `se: null` and `access_tier: abstract_only`.
- No identifier in this shard failed verification, and no record was written on the basis of recall.
